The workspace has a dock on each side of the drawing. Model and Sketch entities start on the left; context properties and Selection start on the right. Panels share their dock's width and scroll within their own bodies.

## Arrange panels

Drag a panel title to an insertion line in either dock. For keyboard movement, focus the title and press Enter, move through the named drop targets with Tab, and press Enter to drop. Escape cancels the move. The title retains focus after moving.

Context panels appear when they are relevant. Sketch entities and Selection return to their saved positions when you enter the sketch editor. In sheet mode, Model shows the sheet list and the properties panel shows sheet settings and selected annotations.

## Fold and restore

The chevron beside a panel title folds its body while keeping its header visible. The corner icon fills the dock with that panel; the inward corners in the same place restore the previous sizes and fold states.

Use the dock's outer chevron to collapse the whole column to an icon rail. Each icon identifies a panel in its current order. Click an icon to open that panel immediately, even if its body was folded. Its restore control returns the dock to its normal arrangement.

## Resize the workspace

Drag the line between expanded panels to transfer height between them. Focus the line and use Up or Down for keyboard resizing. Double-click it to restore default height proportions in that dock.

Drag the canvas-facing edge of a dock to change its width, or focus the edge and use Left or Right. The viewport keeps usable space, and panel headers remain within the window. Resizing the window adjusts the visible allocations without overwriting your preferred proportions.

Dock changes do not fit or zoom the drawing automatically. Use **Fit** after arranging the workspace when you want to frame the drawing in the available viewport.

Your browser remembers panel placement, widths, proportions, folds, and collapsed docks. Temporary fill is not remembered. These preferences are separate from document files and undo history.
